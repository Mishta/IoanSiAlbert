Department:

Structural Engineering  

Physics: 
Understanding basic mechanics, structural forces (tension, compression), and materials science.

Math: 
2D/3D geometry for designing layouts, scaling dimensions, and calculating volumes.

Tools: 
Familiarity with basic 3D modeling software (like CAD, Blender, or Tinkercad) or technical sketching is a plus.

Operations Engineering 

Physics & Chemistry: Concepts related to power generation (solar, nuclear), environmental control, water recycling, and waste management systems.

Logic: 
Creating functional flowcharts for how vital utilities (air, electricity, water) travel through a space station.

Human Factors
Biology & Psychology: Understanding fundamental human needs, safety protocols, atmosphere composition, gravity simulation, and space-suit requirements.

Architecture/Design Creating pleasant community floor plans, residential spaces, and recreational zones for the settlement's residents.

Mission Systems Engineering 

Computer Science:
 Understanding basic automation, logic loops, networks, and communication protocols.

Robotics: Conceptualizing how automated systems and robots will handle external maintenance, construction, or security.
